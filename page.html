Signs your septic needs a visit

Any one of these is worth a call. Two or more, call today.

  • Drains slowing down all over the house
  • Gurgling when you flush or run water
  • A sewage smell inside or out in the yard
  • Soggy or extra-green grass over the tank or drain field
  • Water backing up into a tub, shower or toilet
  • No pump-out in 3+ years, or no idea when the last one was

Common questions

How often should a septic tank be pumped?
Most homes every 3 to 5 years. A smaller tank, a bigger household or a garbage disposal means sooner. If you don’t know when it was last done, it’s probably due.
What if I don’t know where my tank is?
That’s normal, and finding it is part of the job.
Do you offer emergency septic service?
Yes. We work 7 days a week, so a backup on a Saturday doesn’t have to wait for Monday.
