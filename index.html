How the page is built

One job: get a North Atlanta homeowner with a septic need to call. When you give feedback, name the section number.

  1. Header (sticky). Logo left, tap-to-call number right with “7 days a week”.
  2. Hero. Headline matches the ad group (use the dropdown above to see each version). One call button, a callback link, then 4.9 stars from 242 Google reviews, based in Canton, 15+ years.
  3. Sewage coming up a drain? What to do right now, then call.
  4. Signs your septic needs a visit. Six warning signs in customers’ own words.
  5. Services. Pumping and cleaning, repair and installation as cards with the photos from Ray’s current page, plus inspections and emergency service.
  6. What happens when you call. Three steps and the “No hidden fees, no surprises” promise, backed by a customer quote.
  7. Reviews. Four real Google reviews, word for word, plus the HomeAdvisor / Angi / Thumbtack badges.
  8. About. Local septic company in Canton, 15+ years, the techs customers name most.
  9. Where we work. Towns grouped by Ray’s counties, with a map on desktop.
  10. Common questions. How often to pump, finding the tank, emergency service.
  11. Closing. Call button and a 4-field callback form with SMS consent.
  12. Mobile call bar. Fixed red “Call Ray’s now” bar on phones.
